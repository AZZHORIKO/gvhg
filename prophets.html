Qur'anic Narratives of Guidance

While Allah sent many messengers throughout human history, the Holy Qur'an explicitly names 25 prophets. The depth of their narrative varies in scripture: some narratives span entire surahs, while others offer concise, powerful mentions. Each prophet exemplifies a distinct facet of spiritual fortitude, moral leadership, and devotion to pure monotheism (Tawhid).
