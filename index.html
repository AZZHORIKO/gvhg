Personal Reflection Note

Notice that the divine promise repeats ease alongside hardship rather than merely after it. Even in seasons of heavy difficulty, hidden mercies and subtle avenues of strength are already present for the observing heart.

Did You Know?

The Qur'an consists of 114 Surahs (chapters), revealed over a period of 23 years. Surah Al-Baqarah is the longest surah with 286 verses, while Surah Al-Kawthar is the shortest with only 3 verses.
