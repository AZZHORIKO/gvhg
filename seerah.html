Historical & Spiritual Context

Born in Makkah in 570 CE, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ grew up known as Al-Amin (The Trustworthy). Called to prophethood at age 40, his 23-year mission transformed Arabian tribal society through pure monotheism, social justice, and divine guidance.
