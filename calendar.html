Understanding Regional Moon-Sighting Differences

The Islamic Hijri calendar is intrinsically tied to the natural lunar cycle. While standard tabular calculations provide reliable global baselines, local community observances—especially for Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, and Eid al-Adha—are confirmed by local physical moon sighting or regional Hilal authorities. Manarah provides the offset adjustment control above so you can easily sync your calendar with your local religious authority.

Tip: Always follow your local community or Islamic council for the official start of Ramadan and Eid prayers.
